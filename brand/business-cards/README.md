# Business cards (Vistaprint)

Print-ready files for Vistaprint **Standard Business Cards** (US, 3.5" × 2"), matching the website: the front
keeps the original card layout with the UniFi product lineup filling the right side (cut out from its gray backdrop,
centered so it runs off the top and behind the road stripe, with a soft vignette that fades it into the card color on
the text side like the photo on the original card), and the back uses the dark style of the site's contact section.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `route47-business-card.pdf` | **Upload this.** Both sides: page 1 is the front, page 2 is the back |
| `route47-business-card-front.pdf`, `route47-business-card-back.pdf` | The same, one side per file, for when the uploader asks for each side separately |
| `route47-business-card-front.png`, `route47-business-card-back.png` | 300 dpi images (1083 × 633 px), if you'd rather upload images |
| `route47-business-card-proof.png` | Preview only, **don't upload**. Red line = where the card is cut, dashed blue = safe area |

## Specs these follow

- Upload size (full bleed): **3.61" × 2.11"** (1083 × 633 px at 300 dpi)
- Finished card (trim): 3.5" × 2"
- Safe area, where all text stays: 3.36" × 1.86"
- All text is converted to outlines (no fonts needed), the logo is vector, and nothing uses transparency.
- The product image prints at about 290 dpi, just under the 300 Vistaprint asks for. That's fine at card size, but a
  larger original of the same image would print a little sharper.
- The text and logo stay inside the safe area. The products run off the top, bottom and right edges on purpose, so
  exactly how much of them is cut off can shift slightly from card to card.
- Colors are RGB. Vistaprint converts them to CMYK for printing, so the tan, sage and amber can shift slightly
  from what you see on screen. Check the preview in Vistaprint's checkout before ordering.

## Ordering

1. Vistaprint → Business Cards → **Standard** → *Upload design*.
2. Upload `route47-business-card.pdf`. If it asks which page goes where: page 1 = front, page 2 = back.
3. In the preview, make sure nothing important is cut off at the edges.
4. The back prints **route47lowvoltage.com**, so make sure that domain points at the website before handing the
   cards out.
